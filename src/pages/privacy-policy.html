At Digital Utility Hub, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Browser-Based Processing

We design our tools to process data locally in your browser whenever possible. For tools that process files locally (like the Word Counter or client-side formatters), your data is never uploaded to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, device type, and basic analytics data to improve our services.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We may use cookies to enhance your experience. In the future, we may partner with advertising networks (like Google AdSense) which use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@digitalutilityhub.com.
